## What ElderGuard is

ElderGuard is a free tool that helps you decide whether a phone call might be a scam. During a call, it listens along with you, looks for the patterns scammers use, and shows you a signal: Listening, Caution, or Stop. It can also alert a family member you choose.

ElderGuard is not a person. It is a computer program.

## What ElderGuard is not

- **It is not a guarantee.** ElderGuard can miss a scam. It can also warn you about a call that turns out to be fine. You are always the one who decides whether to keep talking, hang up, or give someone money or information.
- **It is not an emergency service.** If you or someone else is in danger, call 911.
- **It is not the police, your bank, or Adult Protective Services.** If you have been scammed, report it to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov and call the National Elder Fraud Hotline at 833-372-8311. ElderGuard does not report anything on your behalf.
- **It does not work on every phone plan.** Merging calls has to be supported by your carrier. The app checks this with you during setup.
- **It does not work on app-to-app calls** such as WhatsApp or FaceTime.

## How the call check works, and what you agree to

When you use the guardian on a call:

1. Your phone calls the ElderGuard line and you merge the calls.
2. A short notice plays on the call saying the call is protected by ElderGuard. Everyone on the call hears it. **You agree to this notice playing.** It is how we make sure the other person knows.
3. ElderGuard listens to the call in real time. Nothing is recorded and nothing that was said is saved. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains exactly what we keep.

You agree to use the guardian only on calls you are part of. Do not use it to listen to other people's calls.

## Family members (guardians)

If you are linked as a family member:

- You sign in on your own phone with your own number.
- You will get alerts about the calls the senior chose to protect.
- You may be asked to record a few short lines in your voice. Those recordings are used only to play into the senior's call and can be deleted by you at any time.
- You can remove yourself at any time.
