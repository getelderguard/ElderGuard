- We never save what was said on a call.

- You can delete your account from inside the app, or by email.

## What ElderGuard does during a call

When you tap "Check this call," your phone calls the ElderGuard line and you merge the two calls. From then on, ElderGuard listens to the call in real time. A computer turns the speech into text, and another computer program reads that text every few seconds to look for signs of a scam. The app shows you a simple signal: Listening, Caution, or Stop.

A short notice plays on the call so that everyone knows ElderGuard is protecting it.

The audio is handled in memory and is gone when the call ends. Nothing is recorded. The text is used for a few seconds and then discarded. We do not keep a transcript.

Two service companies help with this: one turns speech into text, and one reads the text for scam signs. They receive the audio and text only while the call is happening. We configure them not to keep it. They are listed under "Companies that help us" below.

## Information we keep

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your phone number | It is how you sign in, and how the ElderGuard line knows the call is from you. We store it scrambled (a one-way code), plus the last four digits so you can recognise it. Our sign-in provider (Google Firebase) also holds the number. | Until you delete your account |
| Your name and nickname | So the app can greet you and so your family member knows whose call it is | Until you delete your account |
| Your family member's name, relationship, and phone number (scrambled, plus last four digits) | So they can be linked to your account and get alerts | Until they are unlinked or you delete your account |
| Words or topics you asked us to watch for (for example "Medicare") | To help the scam check | Until you change them or delete your account |
| A record of each protected call: when it started and ended, how long it was, and which signal the app showed. Not what was said. | So you and your family member can look back, and so we can fix problems | 30 days, then deleted automatically |
| Your consent: which version of this policy you accepted, and when | So we know what you agreed to | Until you delete your account |
| Usage counts: how many seconds of speech were processed and roughly what it cost us | So we can report to the people who fund ElderGuard. These counts are separated from your name when you delete your account. | 90 days |
| Your family member's recorded voice lines (a future feature) | So ElderGuard can play their voice into a call when it is time to hang up | Until they delete them or the account is deleted |
| A code that lets us send notifications to your phone | So the app can alert you | Until you sign out or delete your account |
| Technical logs | To find and fix problems. Logs never contain what was said or your phone number. | 30 days |
| Crash reports | To fix crashes. These do not include personal information. | 30 days |
| Backups of our database | So we can recover from an outage | 35 days |

## Information we do not keep

- Call recordings. Never.
- Transcripts of calls. Never.
- Your contacts. The app adds one contact card for ElderGuard, with your permission. It does not read your address book.
- Your location.
- Health or financial details. A call may include them, but we do not store the call.
- Anything for advertising.

## Companies that help us

We use a small number of service providers. Each one receives only what it needs to do its job and is not allowed to use your information for anything else.

| Company | What they do | What they receive |
|---|---|---|
| Google Cloud and Firebase | Run our servers and database, send you the sign-in code by text message, deliver notifications | Your phone number (for sign-in), the information in the table above |
| Twilio | Provides the ElderGuard phone line | The phone call itself, your phone number, call times and length. Twilio keeps its own call log. We do not use Twilio's recording feature. |
| Deepgram | Turns speech into text during a call | The call audio, only during the call |
| Anthropic | Reads the text to look for scam signs | Short pieces of the call text, only during the call |
| Sentry | Collects crash reports | Technical error details, no personal information |

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone else, and we do not share it with data brokers.

## Your family member's information

If you link a family member, they sign in on their own phone with their own number. They can see the same call records you can. They cannot see anything that was said on your calls, because we do not have it. They can remove themselves at any time from their own phone.

## Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Our servers hold your phone number only in scrambled form. Only the ElderGuard software, not people, reads the database in normal operation. There is no such thing as perfect security, and we will tell you if something goes wrong that affects you.
