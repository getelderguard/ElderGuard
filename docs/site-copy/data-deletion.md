# Delete Your ElderGuard Account and Data

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

You can delete your ElderGuard account at any time. This page explains how, what gets deleted, and what we keep for a short while afterward.

## The fastest way: inside the app

1. Open ElderGuard.
2. Tap **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete my account**.
4. Confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. You will be signed out.

Email **[CONTACT EMAIL]** with the subject line "Delete my account." Tell us the phone number you used with ElderGuard.

To make sure it is really you, we will send a one-time code by text message to that phone number. Reply to our email with the code. We will then delete your account and email you to confirm. This usually takes a few days and never more than 30 days.

If you cannot receive text messages at that number any more, tell us in the email and we will find another way to confirm it is you.

## What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

- Your sign-in (your phone number and the account behind it)
- Your scrambled phone number and its last four digits
- Your name and nickname
- Your watch words
- Every record of your protected calls
- The link to your family member, and their scrambled phone number if they were not linked to anyone else
- Any recorded voice lines stored for your account
- The code that lets us send notifications to your phone

This happens immediately when you delete from the app, or as soon as we confirm your emailed request.

## What we keep, and for how long

| What | How long | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Usage counts (seconds of speech processed and what it cost) with your name and account removed | 90 days from when they were created | To report totals to the people who fund ElderGuard. They can no longer be connected to you. |
| Technical logs | 30 days | To fix problems. They never contain your phone number or anything said on a call. |
| Database backups | Up to 35 days | To recover from an outage. Backups are never used to bring a deleted account back. |
| A note that a deletion happened, with the date and the version of the privacy policy you had accepted. No name, no phone number. | 1 year | To show that we honoured your request. |
| Twilio's phone call log (the phone company that runs our line), which includes your phone number and the times and lengths of calls | Twilio's standard period; we delete records older than 90 days on a monthly schedule | Twilio keeps this as the carrier. We do not use it. |

We never had recordings or transcripts of your calls, so there is nothing of that kind to delete.

## Family members

If you are a family member (guardian) and want to stop, you do not need to delete the senior's account. In the app, open the senior's card and tap **Remove me**. Or email [CONTACT EMAIL] from your own phone number. We remove your name, your scrambled phone number, and your recorded voice lines, and we tell the senior's phone that you have been removed.
